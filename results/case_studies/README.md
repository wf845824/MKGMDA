# Case-study rankings

Complete rankings and evidence for the two case studies of the MKGMDA paper (colon and breast neoplasms).

- **Sheets**: holdout_colon / holdout_breast: all 788 HMDD v3.2 miRNAs ranked for the held-out disease (colon: Colonic, Colorectal and Rectal Neoplasms removed together). novel_colon / novel_breast: model trained on all HMDD v3.2 associations; candidates are miRNAs without a benchmark association with the disease (colon family) (455 and 450). tcga_samples: TCGA samples used for differential expression.
- **held_out_v32**: Association with the held-out disease (colon: any of the three diseases) in the HMDD v3.2 benchmark.
- **hmdd_v4 / v4_pmids**: HMDD v4.0 record whose disease name is one of: colon = Colon Adenocarcinoma; Colonic Neoplasms; Colorectal Adenocarcinoma; Colorectal Neoplasms; Colorectal Neoplasms, Hereditary Nonpolyposis; Rectal Neoplasms; colon carcinoma; rectum adenocarcinoma; breast = Bone-Associated Metastatic Breast Cancer; Breast Neoplasms; Breast Neoplasms, Basal Cell; Carcinoma, Ductal, Breast; Her2-Positive Breast Cancer; Inflammatory Breast Neoplasms; Quadruple Negative Breast Cancer; Triple Negative Breast Neoplasms. miRNA names are matched after removing the genomic copy suffix (hsa-mir-16-1 -> hsa-mir-16).
- **dbdemc_lowthroughput_pmids**: dbDEMC 3.0 low-throughput validations for cancer types: colon = colorectal cancer, colon cancer; breast = breast cancer. Matched by miRNA family key (hsa- prefix, arm suffix -5p/-3p and copy suffix removed).
- **tcga_log2fc / tcga_fdr**: GDC miRNA-seq (reads per million) of TCGA-COAD (colon) and TCGA-BRCA (breast) from the UCSC Xena GDC hub; primary tumour (sample type 01) vs solid tissue normal (11): COAD 451 vs 8, BRCA 1091 vs 104 samples. Expression is summed over precursors of one family key and log2(x+1)-transformed; a family is tested if 2^m - 1 >= 1 in either group, where m is the group mean of log2(RPM+1). Two-sided Mann-Whitney U test; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR over all tested families of the project. Empty = not expressed or not measured.
- **n_strong_targets / kegg_pathway_targets / pathway_p / pathway_fdr**: miRTarBase 10 functional MTIs (human) supported by luciferase reporter, western blot or qRT-PCR, matched by family key. Universe: all 3096 genes with such support. Pathway genes: KEGG hsa05210 (colorectal cancer; 69 genes in the universe) and hsa05224 (breast cancer; 115 genes). One-sided hypergeometric test; Benjamini-Hochberg FDR over all candidates of the sheet. Interpretive only: miRTarBase also enters the model.

Generated by `run_case_study.py` and `analysis/case_evidence.py`.
